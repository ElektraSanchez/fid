Book Club Poster

Lastly, this is the only Digital piece in the lineup. It is a poster for Book Club, that I made in a game and simulation class. We were tasked to make a fake poster of a club, which I brought my finished product to them afterwards, which they used for the few years I was there. Nunez was my prior English teacher and head of book club, holding a collection of books, most I had read. She offers the audience to join with a big smile and welcoming demeanor.
