My Art Supplies

This is another traditional drawing which shows the tools I used to draw all these pieces. My glove to protect my hand from pencil lead, my eraser to remove blemishes, my sharpener and my small pencils that are used until they cannot be sharpened any longer. They sit on the desk Infront of my wall of vines and window.
