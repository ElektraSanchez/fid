My Room

This is a traditional drawing of my desk where I do my work and art along with my corkboard where I hang drawings and other fun things on. It sits Infront of a window that looks out into the road where I can see cars pass and people walking as I do homework or draw. I tried to capture the chaos of my space, which has since increased. It’s where I work best.
